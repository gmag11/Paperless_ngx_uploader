What's New in 1.8.5

Highlights:
• Client certificates (mTLS) — present a per-server certificate, with an optional custom CA, to reverse proxies that require mutual TLS (#19).
• "Open with" — send documents from other apps directly to the uploader.
• Unreadable shared files are reported instead of being silently skipped.

Other improvements:
• Editing a server no longer discards its other settings, tags or headers.
• Shared and "Open with" files are no longer uploaded twice after the app is recreated.
• Updated Flutter SDK and dependencies.
• New Android unit tests for intent handling.
